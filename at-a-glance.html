MUP – Medical Union Pharmaceuticals

Bringing high quality and affordable medicines since 1984.

MUP started as a small business in 1984 by launching 5 products. Then the 1st conventional plant was established in Abu-Sultan (Ismailia), since then, MUP has grown significantly in Egypt, Middle East and Africa.

1984 – A small businessMUP launches its first 5 products.
Abu-Sultan, IsmailiaThe 1st conventional plant is established.
Two more factoriesOver the last 3 decades MUP introduced the Penicillin and Cephalosporin plants.
Eli-Lilly’s insulin factoryMUP acquires it: the only multinational insulin factory in the Middle East.

I’d like to introduce myself to you as the new Chairman and Managing Director of MUP. I am very enthusiastic to be leading such an important professional organization. My main objective is to ensure the growth of MUP to build on the success of the last 3 decades & maintain sustainable future for MUP.

Our projected revenue for 2022 is L.E. 2.6 bn. We took bold steps to position MUP in the rank it deserves; We began to execute the four key milestones “Insulin cartridge, L.V.P, S.V.P. & Ophthalmology”.
