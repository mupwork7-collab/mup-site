About MUP

MUP manufacturing power

To achieve our objective, MUP developed four facilities producing more than 200 million units yearly. We chose a strategic location in Abu Sultan, Ismailia, considering three key factors: pollution, humidity and distance from Cairo. MUP has been one of the largest establishments in Egypt since 1988, covering different production lines to develop a wide and diverse pharmaceutical portfolio. We have extensive manufacturing capabilities across global markets focused on operational excellence and efficiency.

Philosophy and strategy

In memory of Prof. Dr. Mahfouz Kassem. The R&D center was established by Prof. Dr. Mahfouz Kassem in 1995, and his philosophy was:

Providing research-based medicines for patients who can’t afford expensive medicines.

Since then, R&D has become the hub of science and technology. MUP will continue to invest in new treatments to offer innovative solutions that cure and improve people’s quality of life.

MUP Global

Export supremacy power

The export department was established in 1992, and since then MUP has been one of the leading companies in exporting globally. As growth rates slow in traditional markets, the development of new export markets is essential to sustain the pharmaceutical industry.

Exporting power

MUP is active in more than 40 countries globally, and is currently operating in Africa, GCC, Far East and EAEU countries and preparing to expand to European countries.
